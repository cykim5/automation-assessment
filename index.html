2. Results

    How the scoring works

    Feasibility (can a robot do it?)

    The average of three ratings. Action is how complex the motion is. Object is how predictable the item being handled is. Environment is how controlled the workspace is. A task is only as easy as these three combined.

    Business fit (is it worth it?)

    60% financial return, based on payback time (system cost divided by yearly labor savings), and 40% strategic value, for things money doesn't capture, like a role you can't hire for or a safety risk.

    What to do with it

    Quick wins are high on both and make good pilots. Strategic bets are valuable but hard, so watch the technology. Easy, low value tasks work but don't pay. Everything else, deprioritize.

    Payback assumes the robot covers all listed hours. Real deployments rarely do on day one, so treat this as a first screen before an on-site assessment, not a final answer.
